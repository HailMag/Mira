Mira ⚡

A private personal assistant that runs on one person's own Mac. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Mira helps its owner with his own calendar, email drafts and budget. With Google Calendar it can:

  • Delete an event on the owner's personal calendar, only after he approves that specific delete by replying to a one-time code sent to his phone.
  • Confirm with Google that the event is really gone.

It never adds people to events, never sends invitations, and never connects any other Google account.
