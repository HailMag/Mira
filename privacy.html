Privacy policy

Mira · last updated October 3, 2026

Mira is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on his own Mac. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What Google data Mira uses

Only the owner's own Google Calendar events, through the Google Calendar API, and the email address of the signed-in account (to confirm it's the owner's personal account). Mira uses this solely to find and delete events the owner has approved, and to confirm they were deleted.

Where it's stored

The Google sign-in is stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's Mac. Calendar data is processed on that Mac. A local record of deleted events (title, time, calendar) is kept on the Mac so a delete can be undone.

Sharing

Mira does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Mira's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, or by deleting the "DarkSwarm: Mira Google Calendar" item from the Mac's Keychain.
